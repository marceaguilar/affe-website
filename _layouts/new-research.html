    How this list is built

    Journal articles come from OpenAlex, restricted to an explicit list of accounting, finance, economics and business journals. Working papers come from Crossref, restricted to SSRN’s DOI prefix.

    A paper is kept only if its title or abstract contains an explicit field-experiment term, such as field experiment, randomized controlled trial, audit study or correspondence study, and only if it sits in one of the four fields above. Descriptions are drawn from the paper’s own abstract.

    Verification. Nothing is published until its DOI resolves in both OpenAlex and Crossref and the two databases report the same title. Records that appear in only one database are dropped.
